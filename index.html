How it works

Learn. Work. Lead.

See the full programme →

1 · Learn

Nine months of full-time training, January to September: food and beverage service, wine, front office, housekeeping, cooking and barista skills.

2 · Work

A four-month placement with partner estates, hotels and restaurants in the valley, where students put their training to work.

3 · Lead

Graduates build careers across the Cape and abroad. Some reach management, and two a year study at ITM College in Austria.
