Our story

From Franschhoek, for Franschhoek.

Franschhoek is one of South Africa's great food and wine destinations, yet many young people who grow up here can't afford the training to work in it. In 2018 Michaela Julian, Tarryn Corlett and Brian Moor set out to change that.

Today we're a registered charitable trust giving young people from the Valley recognised training, real work experience and a path into the industry.

Our objectives
01

Reduce the unemployment rate in South Africa

We train hospitality professionals in the Western Cape to secure jobs with our partners, giving them job stability and a better future.

02

Become a locally and internationally supported and recognised hospitality training academy

Our curriculum teaches advanced management and entrepreneurship through online and practical sessions, leading to accredited qualifications and better job prospects around the world.

Michaela JulianFounder & Trustee
Founder & Trustee

After a decade in Asia in senior roles, my family and I settled in Franschhoek in 2016, revitalising The Corner House Guesthouse. With a background in Tourism & Management from Austria and extensive experience across luxury hotels and business services worldwide, I've focused on enhancing our guesthouse. My husband, a creative in photography and videography, and I share a passion for training and development, aiming to uplift our community through education and employment opportunities, fulfilling our dream of making a positive impact.

Offering South Africans access to globally recognised education is not just a mission; it's a privilege. Shannon FortuinWine Lecturer
Wine Lecturer

My journey into the world of wine began unexpectedly in a library one afternoon, when curiosity led me to pick up an application form marked ‘free’ and ‘study’.
