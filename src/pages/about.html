Our story

From Franschhoek, for Franschhoek.

Franschhoek is one of South Africa's great food and wine destinations, yet many young people who grow up here can't afford the training to work in it. In 2018 Michaela Julian, Tarryn Corlett and Brian Moor set out to change that.

Today we're a registered charitable trust giving young people from the Valley recognised training, real work experience and a path into the industry.

Our objectives
01

Reduce the unemployment rate in South Africa

We train hospitality professionals in the Western Cape to secure jobs with our partners, giving them job stability and a better future.

02

Become a locally and internationally supported and recognised hospitality training academy

Our curriculum teaches advanced management and entrepreneurship through online and practical sessions, leading to accredited qualifications and better job prospects around the world.
