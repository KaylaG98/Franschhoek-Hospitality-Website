FAQ

Questions students ask us.

How do I apply?

Fill in the application form at the bottom of this page. If you're shortlisted, we'll invite you for an interview. If you're accepted, pay the R1,400 deposit by 31 December 2026 to secure your place. Applications for 2027 close on 30 October 2026.

Who can apply?

Young people aged 18 to 35. You'll need Grade 12 with at least a 50% average, but we keep six places each year for applicants who don't have Grade 12 but do have years of work experience.

Do I need hospitality experience?

No. You don't need any experience. We'll teach you everything you need.

How many students do you take?

We take 26 students a year.

What are the fees?

For 2027 there is a once-off registration fee of R2,800 if you're selected: a R1,400 deposit by 31 December 2026 and R1,400 on the first day, 11 January 2027. Monthly school fees are R1,400, due on the 7th of each month.

Is there help with fees?

Unfortunately we can't help with fees, so please make sure you and your family can cover them before you apply. You'll find the full fees above.

Where is the Academy?

Our main campus is on Farm Cabrière, Daniel Hugo Street, Franschhoek. We also have a satellite campus in Prince Albert with the Prince Albert Community Trust.

How long is the course?

Nine months of theory and practical training at the Academy, from January to September. Then a four-month work placement from October to January, putting your training to work in a real business.

What will I learn?

Food & beverage service, wine fundamentals, hotel reception, housekeeping, cooking theory and practical cooking, coffee & barista training, life skills & leadership, Franschhoek concierge, online booking systems, entrepreneurship, social media & personal branding, an introduction to South African brandy, and Lobster Ink online learning.

Who can I speak to?

Call or WhatsApp Shaneill on 081 009 5157, or email shaneill@franschhoekhospitalityacademy.co.za.
