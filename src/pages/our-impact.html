Why we exist

Talent is everywhere. Opportunity isn't.

Nearly half of South Africa's young people are unemployed. In the Franschhoek Valley, young people grow up surrounded by world-class hotels, restaurants and wine estates, but until 2018 there was nowhere local to train for a career in them.

We're a social upliftment project with two goals:

  1. Reduce unemployment in South Africa, and especially in the Cape Winelands.
  2. Give local young people the skills to get hired, and follow their careers to track their success.

Who we reach

Our impact goes well beyond the classroom.

717people trained directly

Unemployed young people from historically disadvantaged communities in the Valley, who join our one-year hospitality programme, plus community members aged 18 and over who take our evening short courses, such as Foundation Wine & Service.

4,200lives touched indirectly
  • The families our graduates provide for, at home and in other parts of South Africa
  • Local hotels, restaurants and wine estates looking for skilled, trained staff
  • South Africa's tourism and hospitality industry
  • Local communities, through the entrepreneurship skills our students learn to start businesses of their own

Class of 2025

Every single graduate found full-time work.

Our Class of 2025 started their four-month work placements in October and graduated in February. Every one of them is now employed full-time, in housekeeping, kitchens, tasting rooms and restaurants across the Cape Winelands.
