Collaborations

We work with visionaries.

We partner with organisations that share our mission to empower young people through education and skills. Working together multiplies our impact and builds stronger solutions for our community.

FRANCO

Franschhoek Resource and Network Co-ordinating Organisation · founded 2005

Founded by Ron Whytock, FRANCO helps bridge the gap between privilege and need in Franschhoek through financial aid, training and development programmes.

Learn more →

Hospice Franschhoek

Founded 1986

Hospice Franschhoek's mission is to enhance lives in the Franschhoek Valley through excellence in palliative care, offered regardless of a patient's financial means.
