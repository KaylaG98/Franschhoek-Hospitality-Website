Our training comes to the Karoo through a partnership in Prince Albert

We have formally partnered with the Prince Albert Community Trust (PACT) to bring the same hospitality training our students receive in Franschhoek to young people in Prince Albert.

A new campus in Prince Albert

The partnership gives rise to the Prince Albert Community Trust Hospitality Academy (PACTHA), a new campus in Prince Albert that mirrors the standards of the Franschhoek Hospitality Academy.

We will provide our full curriculum and academic guidance, and support student adjudication. PACTHA will run the programme locally, so that students in Prince Albert receive the same standard of training as students on our Franschhoek campus.

Why it matters

Bringing quality hospitality training to rural areas creates job opportunities, changes lives and strengthens local economies. In Franschhoek, 587 young people have graduated from the Academy since 2018, and 96% of our graduates are in work. We look forward to seeing the same impact in the Karoo.
