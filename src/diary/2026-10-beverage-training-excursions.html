Beverage training at Oldenburg Vineyards and Cape Brewing Company

Our students went behind the scenes of two of South Africa's exciting beverage industries, wine and craft beer.

As part of their ongoing Beverage Training, our students recently had the incredible opportunity to visit Oldenburg Vineyards and Cape Brewing Company, gaining valuable first-hand exposure to two of South Africa's exciting beverage industries.

These educational excursions are about so much more than simply tasting wine and beer. They give our students the opportunity to see, experience and understand the industry from the inside: from production and presentation to service, tasting and the guest experience.

For aspiring Bartenders, Waiters and Tasting Room Ambassadors this exposure is invaluable. It connects classroom theory with real-world practice and helps our students develop the knowledge, confidence, professionalism and industry awareness they need to thrive.

Thank you to everyone who makes days like these possible.
