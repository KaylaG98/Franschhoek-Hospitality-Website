Congratulations, Class of 2025

On 11 February we celebrated not just graduates, but future Hoteliers, Chefs, Sommeliers and leaders who have mastered the art of service with heart.

Your journey has been filled with early mornings, late nights, practicals, placements and unforgettable moments of teamwork and growth, and you've emerged stronger, skilled and ready to shine.

As you step into the world of Hospitality, may you welcome every guest with confidence, lead with professionalism, and create experiences that leave lasting impressions. The industry is brighter because of you.

Michaela Julian, Tarryn Corlett, Brian Moor and Jeremy Davids at the ceremony.

Congratulations, Class of 2025: your story of excellence and service begins now.
