# Monthly website update checklist

Send Claude the notes and photos for each item below. Claude makes the changes, runs `python3 build.py`, and publishes.

## 1. Academy Diary post ("what happened this month")
- [ ] Headline and a two-line summary
- [ ] 3–6 short paragraphs (or bullet notes; Claude can write them up for you to approve)
- [ ] 1 cover photo plus any extra photos, each with a line saying who or what is in it
- [ ] Optional: a quote from a student, trainer or partner, with their name and role
- [ ] Category: Monthly update, Student stories, Events or Partners

Claude copies `src/diary/_TEMPLATE.html` to `src/diary/YYYY-MM-short-name.html`, fills it in, adds a card to the Academy Diary page and makes it the featured post.

## 2. "Where are they now?" graduate spotlight
- [ ] Graduate's name, graduation year and where they work now
- [ ] 2–3 sentences in their own words (with their permission to publish)
- [ ] A photo (with their permission)

## 3. Home page story refresh
- [ ] Which story to feature this month (usually the spotlight above)
- [ ] One strong photo

## 4. Housekeeping
- [ ] Any dates that have changed (intake, open days, events, application deadline)
- [ ] Any new or departing sponsors (send the new logo)
- [ ] Any change to fees, contact details or team members
- [ ] Check the impact numbers are still current (graduates, % in work, community reached)

## 5. After publishing
- [ ] Open the site on your phone and check the new post and home page
- [ ] Share the Diary post on Instagram and Facebook
