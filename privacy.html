Privacy policy

Your privacy matters to us.

This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website, why we collect it, who we share it with and what your rights are under South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Last updated: November 2026

Who we are

The Franschhoek Hospitality Academy & Learning Centre ("the Academy", "we", "us") is a registered charitable trust (IT000107/2017(C)) and public benefit organisation. We are the responsible party for the personal information described in this policy.

What we collect and why

When you apply to study with us

Our application form asks for your names, nickname, gender, date of birth and age, ID number, cellphone number, email address, physical address, a relative's contact number, your school and education, your work and hospitality experience, your answers to our application questions, and your parent or guardian's name.

We use this only to assess your application, contact you about it (for example to invite you for an interview) and, if you are accepted, to enrol you and manage your studies with us.

When you contact us

Our contact form asks for your name, email address, phone number (optional) and your message. We use these only to reply to you and help with your request.

When you sign up for the Academy Diary

We collect your email address to send you our monthly newsletter. You'll get an email asking you to confirm before you're signed up, and every newsletter has an unsubscribe link.

When you donate

Donations are made through SnapScan, PayPal or your own bank. We never see or store your card details. If you ask us for a Section 18A tax certificate, we keep the details needed to issue it (such as your name, the amount and date of your donation), as tax law requires.

When you use the "Ask a question" assistant

The assistant runs entirely in your web browser. Your questions are not sent to us or stored anywhere.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We only share it with service providers who help us run the website and communicate with you:

  • FormSubmit delivers messages from our contact and application forms to our email inbox.
  • MailerLite stores newsletter subscribers' email addresses and sends the Academy Diary.
  • Our email and website hosting providers store our emails and host this website.

Some of these providers store information outside South Africa. By submitting a form on this website, you agree to your information being transferred to them for the purposes above. We only use providers that protect personal information to a standard similar to POPIA.

Cookies and outside services

This website does not use its own cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Some pages include content from other companies, which may collect information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts: your browser downloads our fonts from Google.
  • Google Maps: the map on our Contact page is provided by Google and may set cookies.
  • YouTube: when you play a YouTube video, it is shown in YouTube's player, which may set cookies.
  • SnapScan, PayPal, Instagram, Facebook and partner websites: links open those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it for, or for as long as the law requires. After that we delete it or remove anything that identifies you.

How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to keep your information safe, including limiting who can see it to the people at the Academy who need it.

Your rights

Under POPIA you have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • ask us to correct or delete it;
  • object to us using it, or withdraw your consent;
  • unsubscribe from our newsletter at any time.

To use any of these rights, contact our Information Officer using the details above. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Regulator at inforegulator.org.za.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
